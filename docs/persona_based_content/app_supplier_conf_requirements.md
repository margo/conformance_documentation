# Application Supplier Conformance Requirements

Specification baseline: **v1.0.0-rc.3 (PR3)**, tag `plugfest-2026-10`.

Requirements are extracted from the normative (BCP 14 uppercase keyword) statements in the PR3 specification.
Rows prefixed with *(derived)* come from schema tables that mark a field as required rather than from an uppercase keyword in prose.
CR-IDs follow the [CR-ID specification](../conformance_test_toolkit/crid-specification.md).
Statements not yet promoted to CR-IDs are listed under [Candidate Requirements (for review)](#candidate-requirements-for-review).
Requirements that bind no product persona are in [Non-Persona Requirement Candidates](non_persona_requirement_candidates.md).
