# Non-Persona Requirement Candidates

Specification baseline: **v1.0.0-rc.3 (PR3)**, tag `plugfest-2026-10`.

Statements in the PR3 specification that bind an operator, deployment, identity profile author, or the specification process rather than an Application, Device, or Workload Fleet Manager supplier product.
They are not directly testable by the Conformance Test Toolkit and are listed for discussion: keep as deployment guidance, reassign to a persona, or raise as a specification issue.

Statements the PR3 extraction did not promote to a CR-ID, surfaced for discussion, filtering, and improvement.
Candidate IDs are temporary review handles and are not stable CR-IDs.

| Category | Meaning |
|:--|:--|
| Lowercase | Normative intent expressed with lowercase or non-keyword wording |
| Table-required | Required only through a schema table column |
| OpenAPI-only | Stated in the OpenAPI document but not in the rendered specification pages |
| Non-product | Binds an operator, deployment, or the specification process rather than a product |
| Informative | Descriptive text judged not to be a requirement, listed for confirmation |
| Open item | TBD, Action, or Decision Needed callout in the specification |
